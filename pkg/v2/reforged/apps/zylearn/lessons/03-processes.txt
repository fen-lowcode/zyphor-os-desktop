# Processes: programs that are running

Every running program is a process with a number called a PID.

Try it:
  ps aux | head       list running processes
  top                 live view (press q to quit)
  sleep 300 &         start a background process
  jobs                see it in your shell's job list
  pgrep sleep         find the PID by name
  kill <PID>          politely ask a process to stop (SIGTERM)
  kill -9 <PID>       force it to stop (last resort)
  echo $$             the PID of your current shell
