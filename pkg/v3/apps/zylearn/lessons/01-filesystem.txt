# The Linux filesystem: where everything lives

Linux has ONE tree that starts at "/" (called root). There are no C: or D: drives.

Key directories:
  /home      your personal files (e.g. /home/yourname)
  /etc       system-wide configuration files
  /usr/bin   most installed programs
  /var/log   logs written by the system and services
  /tmp       temporary files, cleared on reboot

Try it:
  pwd                 where am I right now?
  ls -la              list files, including hidden ones (starting with ".")
  cd /etc && ls       go to /etc and look around
  cd                  with no argument, jump back to your home directory
